Tracking and this dashboard work on their own, but writing the journey onto a Person or Deal's custom fields in Pipedrive needs one more thing: a webhook, so Pipedrive tells us the moment a Person or Deal exists.

Important: this doesn't create Person or Deal records in Pipedrive — it only enriches ones that already exist. Your website's own contact form (or however leads reach you) still needs to create the Pipedrive Person itself; this just fills in where they came from once it does.

Tracking social traffic

Paid social (Facebook/Instagram Ads, LinkedIn Ads, etc.) works the same way as Google/Bing Ads: add UTM parameters to the ad's destination URL in the ad platform itself (e.g. ?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=...). Facebook and Instagram click IDs (fbclid) are also captured automatically when the platform appends one, the same way gclid is for Google Ads.

Organic social (someone clicks a link in a LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram/X/TikTok post, with no ad or UTM involved) is detected automatically from the referring site and shown as its own "Organic social" channel — no setup needed. The one thing worth knowing: several platforms' in-app browsers (particularly Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok on mobile) strip the referrer for privacy, so a real organic-social click can occasionally show up as generic website traffic instead. For guaranteed accuracy — especially for a bio link or a post you'll want reliable numbers on — add UTM parameters to that link too (e.g. ?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch-post), the same as you would for an email link.
